# project-document-templates

## Purpose

This repository holds reusable project-document templates.

The first template family is product briefs, because the existing project
folders contain many product brief-like documents that are inconsistent, aimed
at mixed audiences, and often overloaded with history, roadmap, governance,
delivery notes, and status archaeology.

The project is deliberately broader than product briefs. Product briefs are the
first use case, not the whole empire. Future template families may cover
research briefs, decision records, project briefs, review packets, roadmap
summaries, counsel-facing notes, release packets, or other recurring documents
that need a consistent shape.

## Current Scope

- Define a standard product-brief template family by audience.
- Separate product intent from history, roadmap, decisions, risks, and delivery
  tracking.
- Provide templates that are short enough to use and structured enough to keep
  future projects from becoming Markdown compost heaps.
- Keep the repository open to other project-document template families.

## Working Rules

- One document, one job.
- Write for the reader who must make or support a decision.
- Put historical narrative in history or decision logs, not in product briefs.
- Put roadmap detail in roadmap documents, not in product briefs.
- Put implementation plans in delivery docs, not in market/customer briefs.
- Mark draft, proposed, approved, and superseded states plainly.
